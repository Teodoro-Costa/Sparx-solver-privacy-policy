Solver for Sparx Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

What we collect

Solver for Sparx does not collect any personal data. We never receive your name, email address, Sparx account, questions, answers or API key. The only thing the extension sends to us is the anonymous usage count described below.

Anonymous usage statistics

Once a day, the extension sends anonymous usage counts to our server: a random ID created when the extension is installed (not linked to you or your device), the extension version, which AI provider is selected (Groq, Gemini or Claude), and how many times each feature was used (for example Solve, Auto-fill or the calculator). This helps us see how many people use the extension and which features work well.

We do not store IP addresses, and no question text, answers or page content is ever included. Only combined totals are used. The server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the connection on our behalf.

Local storage

Your API key, model preference, settings, and answer/bookwork history are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your browser.

AI providers

When you solve a question, the question text and/or screenshot is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected (Groq, Google Gemini, or Anthropic) using your own API key. We do not proxy, log, or intercept these requests. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Website content

The extension reads question text and images from Sparx Maths pages in order to solve them. This content is only used to generate answers and is not stored or shared beyond the AI API call.

Sponsored banner

The side panel shows a small sponsored banner loaded from our website. We do not send any of your data to it.

Third parties

We do not sell, transfer, or share any user data with third parties. Usage statistics are used only to maintain and improve the extension.
