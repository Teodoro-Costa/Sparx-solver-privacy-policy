Privacy Policy

Solver for Sparx

Solver for Sparx is a Chrome extension that helps with Sparx Maths questions. You can use it for free with your own AI key, or subscribe to Pro (Base or Plus) so you don't need a key. This page explains what data is used in each case.

In short: we never store your questions or answers, we never see your card details, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

1. Free mode (your own API key)

Your API key, settings and answer history are saved only in your browser. When you solve a question, the question text and a screenshot of it go straight from your browser to the AI company you picked (Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic). We are not involved in that request and receive nothing from it.

2. Pro mode (Base and Plus)

Your account. To use Pro you sign up with an email address and a password. We store your email address; your password is stored only in hashed form, so we can't read it.

Your subscription. We store your plan (Base or Plus), whether you pay monthly or yearly, whether it's active, when it renews or ends, and the reference numbers Stripe gives us for it.

Solving questions. When you solve a question with Pro, the question text and screenshot are sent to our server, which checks your plan and passes them to Anthropic's Claude AI for the answer. We do not save the question, the screenshot or the answer. We only record:

  • how many questions you have used this week, so the weekly limits work, and
  • for each question: the time, whether it was a graph question, which AI model answered and how much it cost us.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe and sold through Link, Stripe's checkout service. Link is the seller, handles VAT and sends your receipts. We never see or store your card details. Stripe tells us what we need to run your subscription, such as your email address, your country and whether a payment went through.

4. Anonymous usage statistics

Once a day, the extension sends us anonymous counts: a random ID made when you install it (not linked to you or your Pro account), the extension version, which mode you use (Gemini, Groq, Claude or Pro), and how often each feature was used. It never includes your questions, answers or anything from the page, and we don't store IP addresses.

5. Reading Sparx pages

The extension reads the question text and pictures on Sparx Maths pages so it can solve them. This is only used to get the answer.

6. Sponsored banner

The side panel shows a small sponsored banner loaded from our website. None of your data is sent to it.

7. Services we use

We only share data with these services, and only so the extension works:

  • Supabase — stores Pro accounts and subscriptions
  • Cloudflare — runs our server
  • Anthropic — the AI that answers Pro questions (privacy policy)
  • Stripe and Link — payments (privacy policy)

Some of these process data outside the UK and EU, including in the United States.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your account and subscription details until you delete your account. When you do, your email address, subscription and weekly counts are deleted, and the cost records are no longer linked to you. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account by emailing us at the contact address on our Chrome Web Store listing. Please cancel your subscription in the extension first so you aren't charged again. If you're in the UK, you can also complain to the ICO.

10. Age

You must be 13 or older to create a Pro account. If you're under 13, a parent or guardian needs to set it up.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page.

12. Contact

Questions about your data? Use the contact email on our Chrome Web Store listing.
